    Tests and Open Questions

    You can find below a set of anonymous Google Forms on a subset of the topics of this course.

    • "Quiz" are multiple-choice questions that have nothing to do with the exam but that may be useful for checking and for consolidating your understanding of the corresponding topic. You will receive a summary of correct/wrong answers automatically upon submitting the form.
    • "Open questions" are similar to the questions you could find in the exam (onlly similar). You will not receive any assessment. I might try to provide some feedback but I cannot promise it.

    Of course, all forms are anonymous.

    I have generated all the questions with an AI tool but I have checked them all, one by one. Should you find any ambiguities or claims that you do not agree with, please do not hesitate to tell me.
